Reliability rules

Interpreters set their own availability, so they're accountable for it. Penalties only change ranking order. If a deprioritized interpreter is the only one free, they're still offered.

Cancel 24+ hours beforeNo penalty
Cancel 12–24 hours beforeWarning on record (3 warnings count as one late cancellation)
Cancel under 12 hours beforeMoved to the middle of the list for the next cycle
Leave mid-appointmentMoved to the end of the list for the next cycle; patient handed to on-demand video instantly
No-showMoved to the end of the list for 3 cycles

Audit log

Every action by the agent, rule checks, the clinic, interpreters and the patient. This is the record that shows effective communication was provided.

Demo data only: every patient, interpreter and clinic here is fictional. Availability is understood with the browser's built-in speech recognition and a rule-based phrase parser. Matching uses transparent weighted scoring after hard credential rules. No external AI service is called and no data leaves this page.
