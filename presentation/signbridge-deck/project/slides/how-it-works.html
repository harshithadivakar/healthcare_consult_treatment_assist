SignBridge care journey

How it works: one portal, three people, four stages

1 · BEFORE

Pre-consultation

Doctor sends a questionnaire.

Patient records answers in BSL and presses Send.

Interpreter is matched, interprets and returns it within the agreed window.

2 · DURING

Consultation

Patient picks a recommended interpreter, online or in person.

Doctor consults; notes are saved for the patient.

No-show? Speech-to-text and text-to-speech keep the appointment. Interpreter drops to last priority.

3 · AFTER

Post-consultation

Doctor sends follow-up questions.

Patient replies in BSL or text.

Interpreter returns it to the doctor in the same window.

4 · CLOSE

Rate and delete

Patient and doctor rate the interpreter; the interpreter rates them both.

7 days to raise a ticket or complaint.

Then everything is deleted.

MATCHING

Interpreters are ranked by declared availability, ratings from past patients and doctors, experience with similar treatment histories and reliability. The patient makes the final choice.

Walk the four columns left to right. Pre-consultation: the doctor sends a questionnaire, the Deaf patient records BSL answers and presses send, and the platform matches an available BSL interpreter who interprets and returns it within the agreed window. Consultation: the patient chooses from interpreters recommended for patients with similar treatment histories and books an online call or in-person visit; notes are captured in the app. If the interpreter does not turn up, the patient switches to speech-to-text and text-to-speech so the appointment is not wasted, and the interpreter drops to last priority for matching. Post-consultation mirrors pre-consultation. Finally everyone rates each other, and all information is kept for 7 days for tickets or complaints, then deleted.
